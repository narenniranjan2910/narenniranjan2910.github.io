About

From pixels to picks — the story behind the systems.

I build AI-based perception for industrial automation — real-time computer vision, sensor fusion, SLAM and robot programming across FANUC, ABB and Yaskawa platforms. My work sits at the point where a research paper has to become a production line: models that must run at 30 FPS on an embedded GPU, calibrations that must hold to sub-centimeter accuracy shift after shift, and robots that must not miss.

I started in mechanical and automation engineering, programming Siemens and Omron PLCs and Yaskawa robots for production lines — so I understand automation from the actuator up. Since then I've moved through ROS 2 autonomous navigation, LiDAR SLAM and machine vision for AMRs and manipulators, and I'm now completing an M.Sc. in Robotics (Mechatronics & Physical AI) at the University of Twente.

My master's thesis at AWL-Techniek replaces expensive RGB-D cameras with standard industrial RGB cameras, using monocular depth estimation deployed on the NVIDIA Jetson Thor to drive FANUC picking and palletizing.

I'm actively looking for roles in robotics software, computer vision, AI-based perception and industrial automation — anywhere the problem is making machines see and act reliably.

RGB-Only AI Perception for Industrial Robotic Picking

Problem

RGB-D cameras are expensive and restrictive for industrial deployment. The thesis investigates whether standard industrial RGB cameras, paired with AI depth estimation, can drive robotic picking and palletizing of packages on a moving conveyor.

My role

End-to-end owner: model selection and optimization, embedded deployment, multi-camera calibration, point cloud fusion, pose estimation, and integration with the FANUC robot cell.

Technical challenges
  • Packages moving on a live conveyor
  • Occlusion between stacked packages
  • Monocular depth ambiguity and scale
  • Motion blur at line speed
Solution

A 3-camera synchronized rig with intrinsic, extrinsic and hand-eye calibration feeds depth models exported through PyTorch → ONNX → TensorRT on the Jetson Thor. Semantic segmentation isolates packages, fused point clouds recover geometry, and estimated poses stream to FANUC pick-and-place workflows.

Results
30 FPS real-time Sub-cm localization Industrial deployment feasibility
